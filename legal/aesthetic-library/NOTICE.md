Changes: rewritten in Chinese for physical spaces, signs, exhibitions and custom
objects; connected to DSH native skill retrieval, editable geometry review and
Blender output. Web-only measurements, upstream tools, installation, browser
automation, agent orchestration and telemetry flows are not included. No fonts,
brand images or upstream executable code are distributed here.

The references are an adapted method library, not the complete original skills
and not evidence of improved design quality. Pinned paths and SHA-256 values
are recorded in `provenance.json`.
